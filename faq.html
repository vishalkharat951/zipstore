Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, go to Checkout, enter your shipping details, choose a payment method (UPI / PhonePe), and complete payment. You can shop as a guest or with an account.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI payments through the PhonePe gateway (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, BHIM and other UPI apps). Card and net banking options are available on the PhonePe payment page.

I paid but my order still says "Payment Pending". What now?

Sometimes the payment confirmation takes a few minutes. Visit My Orders and refresh, or use the Track Order page. If it still shows pending after 30 minutes, contact Support with your order ID and transaction reference.

How can I track my order?

Use the Track Order page with your order ID, or sign in and visit My Orders.

What is the delivery time?

Metro cities: 2–4 business days. Other locations: 4–7 business days. See the Shipping Policy for details.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, you can cancel before dispatch from My Orders. See the Cancellation Policy.

How do refunds work?

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. See the Refund Policy.

I received a damaged or wrong item. What do I do?

Contact Support within 7 days of delivery with your order ID and photos. We'll arrange a return, replacement, or refund.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by PhonePe, a regulated payment gateway. We never store your card or UPI credentials. All traffic uses HTTPS encryption.

Still have questions?

Visit the Support page or email support@zipstore.in.
