Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about orders, payments, shipping, returns, and refunds.

Last updated: 09 August 2026
1

Orders & Payment

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, go to Checkout, enter your shipping details, choose a payment method (UPI via PhonePe), and complete payment. You can shop as a guest or with an account.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI payments through the PhonePe gateway (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, BHIM and other UPI apps). Card and net banking options are available on the PhonePe payment page.

I paid but my order still says "Payment Pending". What now?

Sometimes the payment confirmation takes a few minutes. Visit My Orders and refresh, or use the Track Order page. If it still shows pending after 30 minutes, contact Support with your order ID and transaction reference.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by PhonePe, a regulated payment gateway. We never store your card or UPI credentials. All traffic uses HTTPS encryption.

2

Shipping & Tracking

How can I track my order?

Use the Track Order page with your order ID, or sign in and visit My Orders.

What is the delivery time?

We aim to deliver your order within 7 days, or by the date agreed at order confirmation. Delivery times depend on our courier or postal partner and are subject to their service norms. See the Shipping Policy.

Do you charge for shipping?

No shipping charge is added at checkout, so your order ships free of charge. If any shipping costs are ever levied, they are non-refundable. See the Shipping Policy.

3

Returns, Refunds & Cancellations

What is the return policy?

Returns are accepted within 7 days of purchase. See the Return Policy for the exact rule.

How do refunds work?

In case of any refund approved by the company, it will take 7 days for the refund to be credited to your account. See the Refund Policy.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, you can cancel before dispatch. See the Cancellation Policy for the exact rule.

I received a damaged or wrong item. What do I do?

Contact Support within 7 days of purchase with your order ID and details. Your case will be reviewed under our Return Policy and Refund Policy.
