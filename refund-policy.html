Refund & Return Policy

Everything you need to know about returning an item and getting your money back.

Last updated: 08 August 2026
Quick Summary

You can return any eligible item within the first 7 days from the date of purchase. After 7 days, returns are no longer offered. In case of any refunds approved by the company, it will take 7 days for the refund to be credited to your account.

7Days

Return Window — First 7 Days

We offer Return within the first 7 days from the date of your purchase. If 7 days have passed since your purchase, you will not be offered a return.

To be eligible, items must be unused and in their original packaging, along with the order details.

1

When Can You Return?

  • Damaged, defective, or wrong item: return the item within 7 days of purchase for a full refund or replacement.
  • Cancelled orders: if you cancel before dispatch (see Cancellation Policy), we refund the full amount.
  • Failed or duplicate payment: if your payment is captured but your order is not confirmed, we refund the full amount automatically.
  • Non-delivery: if your order is not delivered within the promised timeframe, we investigate and refund where applicable.
2

Non-Returnable / Non-Refundable Items

  • Personalised or made-to-order items that have been manufactured as per your specifications.
  • Products that have been used, altered, or damaged by the customer.
  • Digital/downloadable products once accessed.
3

How to Request a Return or Refund

Contact our support team via the Support page or email support@zipstore.in within the 7-day window, providing your order ID and reason.

4

Refund Method & Timeline

In case of any refunds approved by the company, it will take 7 days for the refund to be credited to your account.

  • Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method (UPI / card / net banking) using our payment partner.
  • Payments made via PhonePe UPI are returned to the original account.
5

Return Shipping

For eligible returns, we provide a return label or reimburse standard return shipping costs. Items should be returned in original packaging.

6

Contact

Questions about returns or refunds? Email support@zipstore.in or reach us via the Contact page.
