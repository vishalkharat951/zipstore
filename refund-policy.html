Refund Policy

How approved refunds are processed and when you can expect the money back in your account.

Last updated: 09 August 2026
Quick Summary

If a refund is approved by the company, the refund will be credited to your account within 7 days.

1

Approved Refunds

In case of any refunds approved by the company, it will take 7 days for the refund to be credited to your account.

2

How Refunds Are Credited

Approved refunds are credited back to the account you used for the payment. If you paid via UPI through the PhonePe payment gateway, the refund is returned to the same account.

3

Refund Timeline

Refund Approved

Your refund is approved by the company.

Processing

Your refund is processed back to your account.

Credit to Your Account

Within 7 days of approval.

4

Related Policies

Refunds are closely related to our Return Policy and Cancellation Policy. You can also read how orders are delivered in the Shipping Policy.

5

Questions?

Email support@zipstore.in or reach us via the Contact page. Include your order ID so we can help faster.
