Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 05 August 2026

1. When Can You Cancel?

  • Before dispatch: you can cancel any order before it is shipped. A full refund will be issued.
  • After dispatch: once an order is shipped, cancellation is not possible online. You may decline the delivery or request a return after delivery.

2. How to Cancel

Visit My Orders and use the cancel option on the eligible order, or contact support via the Support page with your order ID.

3. Automatic Cancellations

We may cancel an order automatically if:

  • Payment is not received or verified within a reasonable time.
  • The product is out of stock.
  • A pricing or information error is detected.
  • Suspected fraud or unauthorised activity is detected.

4. Refunds for Cancellations

Cancelled orders are refunded to the original payment method. Refunds typically reflect within 5–10 business days. See the Refund Policy for details.

5. Contact

Need help cancelling? Email support@zipstore.in.
