Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about how your orders are delivered.

Last updated: 08 August 2026
Quick Summary

Orders are shipped via registered domestic courier companies and/or speed post, delivered within 7 days (or the date agreed at confirmation) subject to courier norms. Delivery is made to the address provided at purchase, confirmed by email, and any shipping costs levied are non-refundable.

1

Delivery Method & Areas

The orders for the user are shipped through registered domestic courier companies and/or speed post only. We currently deliver across India — availability for your pincode is confirmed during checkout.

2

Delivery Timeframe

Orders are delivered within 7 days from the date of the order and/or payment, or as per the delivery date agreed at the time of order confirmation and delivery of the shipment, subject to courier company / post office norms.

StageIndicative Timeframe
Order processing & packing1–2 business days after payment confirmation
Metro cities2–4 business days
Other locations4–7 business days
Maximum delivery windowWithin 7 days (subject to courier / post office norms)
3

Courier Delays & Liability

Platform Owner shall not be liable for any delay in delivery by the courier company / postal authority. If your order is delayed beyond the estimated timeframe, contact us via the Support page and we will investigate with the logistics partner.

4

Delivery Address

Delivery of all orders will be made to the address provided by the buyer at the time of purchase. Please ensure your delivery address and phone number are correct at checkout — we are not responsible for deliveries to incorrectly provided addresses.

5

Delivery Confirmation

Delivery of our services will be confirmed on your email ID as specified at the time of registration. You can also track your order anytime using the Track Order page or by visiting My Orders.

6

Shipping Charges

  • Standard shipping is FREE on all orders at present.
  • No hidden charges — the amount shown at checkout is the final amount.
  • If there are any shipping cost(s) levied by the seller or the Platform Owner (as the case may be), the same is not refundable.
7

Contact

Shipping questions? Email support@zipstore.in or reach us via the Contact page.
