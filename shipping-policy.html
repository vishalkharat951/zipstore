Shipping Policy

How your orders are shipped, delivered, and confirmed.

Last updated: 09 August 2026
Quick Summary

Orders are shipped through registered domestic courier companies and/or Speed Post, delivered within 7 days (or the date agreed at order confirmation) subject to courier/post office norms, to the address provided at purchase.

1

Shipping Method

Orders are shipped through registered domestic courier companies and/or Speed Post only.

2

Delivery Timeline

Orders are delivered within 7 days from the date of the order and/or payment, or according to the delivery date agreed at the time of order confirmation and delivery of the shipment, subject to courier company / post office norms.

3

Delivery Address

Delivery of all orders will be made to the address provided by the buyer at the time of purchase. Please make sure the delivery address is correct at checkout.

4

Delivery Confirmation

Delivery of our services will be confirmed on your email ID as specified at the time of registration. You can also check the latest status anytime on the Track Order page or My Orders.

5

Delivery Delays

The Platform Owner shall not be liable for any delay in delivery caused by the courier company / postal authority. If your order is delayed, contact us via the Support page and we will investigate with the logistics partner.

6

Shipping Charges

If there are any shipping cost(s) levied by the seller or the Platform Owner (as the case may be), the same is not refundable.

7

Questions?

Shipping questions? Email support@zipstore.in or reach us via the Contact page.
