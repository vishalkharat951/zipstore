Shipping Policy

Last updated: 05 August 2026

1. Delivery Areas

We currently ship across India. Delivery availability for your pincode is checked during checkout.

2. Shipping Charges

  • Standard shipping is FREE on all orders.
  • No hidden charges — the amount shown at checkout is the final amount.

3. Order Processing Time

  • Orders are packed within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation.
  • Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

4. Delivery Timeframes

  • Metro cities: 2–4 business days.
  • Other locations: 4–7 business days.
  • Estimated delivery dates shown on the order page are indicative.

5. Tracking Your Order

You can track your order at any time using the Track Order page or by visiting My Orders.

6. Delayed or Lost Shipments

If your order is delayed beyond the estimated timeframe, contact us via the Support page and we will investigate with the logistics partner.

7. Address Accuracy

Please ensure your delivery address and phone number are correct at checkout. We are not responsible for deliveries to incorrectly provided addresses.

8. Contact

Shipping questions? Email support@zipstore.in.
